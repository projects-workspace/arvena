      Methodology

      The basis stays visible

      Arvena separates source types, states uncertainty, and shows limitations before asking a visitor to act.

      01

      Define the need

      Begin with the user’s practical problem and the local conditions that affect it.

      02

      Check the basis

      Use relevant public-health guidance, technical standards, product documentation, and transparent testing.

      03

      Name the limits

      Do not turn a category-level finding into a claim for every product. Show missing data and maintenance needs.

      04

      Review before access

      Publish a seller or product link only after its claims, market availability, and commercial relationship are checked.

      Evidence labels

      Context, not a universal score

      Public-health guidance
      Guidance from an accountable public-health body.
      Technical standard
      A defined testing or certification framework; the exact claim still matters.
      Product documentation
      Manufacturer information that requires independent checking where material.
      Arvena reviewed
      The page and its limitations were editorially checked on the stated date.
      Scope and safety

      Arvena provides educational product-discovery information, not diagnosis or medical treatment. During advisories, emergencies, or uncertainty about drinking-water safety, follow the responsible local authority or a qualified professional.
