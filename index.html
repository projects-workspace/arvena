      Marketplace methodology

      How Arvena supports marketplace selection

      Arvena’s evaluation is editorial and evidence-informed. It helps explain and select marketplace offers; it does not replace the marketplace or constitute a formal certification programme.

      01

      Define the need

      Begin with the practical problem and the local conditions that affect a suitable product, service, system or implementation.

      02

      Adapt the criteria

      Evaluate each offer type with relevant criteria instead of applying one universal checklist.

      03

      Check evidence and limits

      Distinguish external standards, technical testing, product documentation and editorial assessment. Keep uncertainty and maintenance needs visible.

      04

      Explain access

      Publish access information only when the specific offer, availability and any commercial relationship can be stated transparently.

      Evidence labels

      Context, not a universal score

      Public-health guidance
      Guidance from an accountable public-health body.
      External technical standard
      A third-party testing or certification framework; certification must be checked for the exact model and claim.
      Product documentation
      Manufacturer information that requires independent checking where material.
      Arvena editorial assessment
      An Arvena interpretation of the page’s evidence and limitations, not a certification, formal verification, or approval of any model.
      Scope and safety

      Arvena provides marketplace selection and educational information, not diagnosis or medical treatment. Limitations, uncertainty and material commercial relationships should remain visible. During advisories, emergencies or uncertainty about drinking-water safety, follow the responsible local authority or a qualified professional.
